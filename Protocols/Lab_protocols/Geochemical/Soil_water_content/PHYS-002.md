## **Procedure**

### **Before Starting**

#### **Checklist**

☐ Required training completed

☐ Reserve drying oven for the duration of the drying steps/ experiment

☐ Equipment available

☐ Sample IDs assigned

### **Step-by-Step Procedure**

1.  Label the weighing boats with your sample labels

2.  Weight the empty weighing boat and note down the weight in a table

3.  Put your fresh soil sample (1 - 1.5 gram) in the allotted weighing
    boat and measure the wet weight including the weighing boat and note
    this down in the table.

4.  Place your samples in the boats into a drying oven at 105°C.

5.  Check the weight of your samples over the following days. Once the
    weight has stabilised you can note the values including the boat
    weight down as dry weight.

6.  Calculate the gravimetric soil moisture by using this equation:

$$Gravimetric\ soil\ moisure\ (\%) = \ \frac{(Wetweight - boat\ weight) - (Dry\ weight - boat\ weight)\ }{(Dry\ weight - boat\ weight)}x\ 100\%$$

## **Critical Control Points**

⚠: Be very careful when moving your samples.

Why it is critical: If any soil is spilled it will ruin your results.

How to avoid mistakes: move carefully and do not move too many samples
at once.
