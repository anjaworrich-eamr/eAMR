## **Purpose**

This method is used to determine the moisture in (soil) samples.

## **Scope**

### **Suitable for**

- Most types of samples including soils, plants, and liquids, as long as
  no corrosive and/ or toxic chemicals are present.

- The weight range of samples that can be measured is: 0.5 g -- 71 g
  (see notes about the standard deviation at point 4 below).

### **Not suitable for**

- Old/ dried out samples

- Any toxic or corrosive chemicals, as this can lead to toxic fumes.

### **Step-by-Step Procedure**

**General information**

- The standard program will heat your sample to 105 degrees Celsius and
  will stop the measurement when your sample is completely dry i.e.
  there is no weight loss measured anymore. There are other options, see
  tips at the bottom for this.

- When using the standard program described above, it takes
  approximately 10-15 minutes for a sample of 2 grams of soil.

- One sample can be measured at a time. After a short cooling period and
  removing the previous sample the next sample measurement can be
  started.

- You will get the most accurate reading if the device has been plugged
  in for at least one hour.

#### **How to use the moisture analyser:**

1.  You can turn on the device by pressing the 'on' button. Check if the
    device is leveled correctly, by making sure that the air bubble is
    in the center of the circle.

2.  Blinking icons on the display will show which step you need to take
    at that moment, as shown below (blinking parts indicated in gray):

3.  Place the sample pan in the holder and make sure that it is level.
    It is important that the sample pan is not bend, use a new one if
    this is the case. New sample pans are stored in a blue box in the
    top drawer below the device. Close the lid to tare (no need to press
    any buttons, it will tare automatically).

> ![](./images/media/image1.png){width="5.352179571303587in"
> height="0.6006080489938758in"}

4.  You can now place your sample in the sample pan. Make sure that your
    sample is spread out evenly over the bottom of the sample pan. The
    standard deviation from larger samples is smaller compared to small
    samples (max. 0.05% for 2g samples, max. 0.01% for 10g samples). So
    it is likely best to use at least 2 g of each sample. The required
    minimum weight of a sample is 0.5g (maximum 71 g).

> ![](./images/media/image1.png){width="5.352179571303587in"
> height="0.46348643919510063in"}

5.  Close the lid.

> ![](./images/media/image1.png){width="5.352179571303587in"
> height="0.7377405949256343in"}

6.  Drying will start automatically.

> ![](./images/media/image1.png){width="5.352179571303587in"
> height="0.7444225721784777in"}

7.  The device will beep as soon as the drying is finished. 'END' will
    be showing in the progress indicator diagram. The display will
    indicate the moisture content (MC) of your sample in %.

> ![](./images/media/image4.png){width="1.5104166666666667in"
> height="1.4375in"}

8.  After cooling you can open the lid by using the sides (indicated
    here by the black arrows). Do not use the gray handle of the sample
    tray holder; you will spill your sample.

> ![](./images/media/image2.png){width="2.15625in" height="1.6875in"}

9.  Take out the sample pan by lifting the sample pan holder. You can
    take the pan out of the holder by lifting it up slightly. Clean the
    sample tray with a tissue. If any sample spilled onto the device
    make sure to clean this too, but be careful some parts might still
    be hot.

10. You can now measure another sample by repeating the previous steps.

**Tips:**

- Your sample will dry quicker if it has a larger surface. You can use
  glass fiber filters to do this for liquids.

- If you do need to measure samples that could produce toxic gases, use
  the device in a fume hood.

- There are other options such as programming a second type of program.
  This can include stopping the drying process after a certain amount of
  time. The device will calculate the moisture using the weight lost
  within this time. This can be useful to make the analyses shorter.
  Please confer the handbook, that can be found here:

> [[https://www.hogentogler.com/images/Mettler_HE73_manual.pdf?srsltid=AfmBOopjCxtCQHZcgqIXAZFmqFY_fN1nG0YPj9t_6cjIsb26p7YJp48s]{.underline}](https://www.hogentogler.com/images/Mettler_HE73_manual.pdf?srsltid=AfmBOopjCxtCQHZcgqIXAZFmqFY_fN1nG0YPj9t_6cjIsb26p7YJp48s)

## **Critical Control Points**

⚠ Step: Balancing the sample pan

Why it is critical: If the pan is not level no accurate measurements can
be taken.

How to avoid mistakes: Make sure that the pan sit horizontally in the
holder and is touching all three arms of the holder.
